Before you ask

Common questions.

Where do you operate?

Pan-India Network. Headquartered in Bengaluru, we provide reliable freight transportation across India, connecting every state and major city through seamless intracity and intercity logistics.

What sizes and vehicle types do you offer?

Flexible Fleet. Perfect Fit. Our diverse fleet includes 8 vehicle sizes from 7 ft to 32 ft, available in Container, Tarpaulin, Tarpaulin + Gate, and Open Body configurations. We right-size every vehicle to your load, optimizing cost, safety, and delivery efficiency.

Do you own your fleet?

Yes. We operate a fleet of our own vehicles while also leveraging a trusted aggregator network. This hybrid model ensures dependable capacity, flexible scaling, and reliable service even during peak demand.

How fast can I get a quote?

Simply send us your requirement details, and we'll get back to you with a customized quote.

What services do you cover?

End-to-End Logistics. Nationwide Reach. From B2B, B2C, and D2C deliveries to First Mile, Mid Mile, Last Mile, Short Mile, and Linehaul transportation, we provide On-Demand and Dedicated logistics solutions for both intracity and intercity movements across India — all managed by one trusted logistics partner.

What type of businesses do you serve?

We provide Dedicated and On-Demand logistics solutions for B2B, B2C, and D2C businesses across India.
