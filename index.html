Who we are

Built on operational expertise.

INAYA Logistics is a technology-driven logistics company delivering reliable, efficient, and scalable transportation across India. Our experience was built on the ground from 2018 onward, and the company was officially incorporated in 2025.

Today we move goods through a combination of owned vehicles and a trusted aggregator network, from our operational base in Bengaluru to destinations across Pan India.
