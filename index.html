Why Choose INAYA Logistics?

More Than Just Transportation

At INAYA Logistics, we are your Single Point of Contact (SPOC) for complete logistics and transportation management. From vehicle sourcing and shipment planning to delivery and post-delivery support, we take full ownership of your logistics operations, allowing you to focus on growing your business.

A Reliable & Efficient Logistics Partner

Shipment Planning

Plan and schedule shipments based on your business requirements, delivery timelines, and operational priorities for seamless execution.

Route Optimization

Optimized routing helps reduce transit time, improve fuel efficiency, and ensure timely deliveries across intracity, intercity, and PAN India networks.

Fleet Management

Our hybrid service model combines own fleet and verified aggregator partners, ensuring vehicle availability, flexibility, and scalable transportation solutions.

Aggregator Onboarding, BGV & Compliance

Every aggregator vehicle, fleet owner, and driver undergoes a stringent onboarding process before joining our network. This includes Background Verification (BGV), comprehensive document verification, KYC validation, and compliance checks for RC, insurance, permits, fitness certificates, PUC, and driving licenses. We also monitor document renewals and regulatory compliance to ensure every vehicle in our network operates safely, legally, and reliably.

Vehicle Allocation

The most suitable vehicle is assigned based on cargo type, shipment volume, destination, and delivery timelines to maximize efficiency and optimize transportation costs.

24/7 Operations & Command Centre

Our dedicated operations team monitors every shipment in real time, coordinates with drivers and transport partners, proactively manages exceptions, and ensures smooth execution from pickup to delivery.

Real-Time Shipment Visibility

Track your shipments with live updates, status notifications, and proof of delivery, providing complete visibility and transparency throughout the transportation journey.
