Who we are

Built on operational expertise.

INAYA Logistics is a technology-driven logistics company committed to providing reliable, efficient, and scalable transportation solutions across India.

Our journey began during 2018 to 2019, building deep operational expertise in the logistics industry. With years of hands-on experience in transportation and supply chain operations, INAYA Logistics was officially incorporated in 2025.

Today, we deliver flexible logistics solutions through a combination of owned vehicles and a trusted aggregator network, enabling businesses to move goods safely, efficiently, and on time. With our operational base in Bengaluru, we provide transportation services across Pan India, ensuring seamless connectivity for businesses of all sizes.
